ContractorSpecialties

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026

Who we are

ContractorSpecialties is software for independent contractors to manage customers, estimates, jobs, invoices, communications, and payments. References to “we” and “our” mean the operator of the ContractorSpecialties platform.

Information we collect

We collect information submitted when creating and using an account, including names, business and contact details, customer records, estimates, job descriptions, schedules, invoices, communications, and payment-related records. We may also collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information, and application activity.

How information is used

We use information to provide and maintain the service, authenticate accounts, communicate about estimates and jobs, process and reconcile payments, deliver requested messages, provide support, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.

Contractor and customer records

Contractors enter customer information into the platform to carry out their business activities. Contractors are responsible for providing appropriate notices, obtaining necessary permissions, and using customer information lawfully. Customers should contact their contractor about the underlying job or service.

Payments and connected accounts

Online payments may be handled by independent payment providers, including Stripe. ContractorSpecialties may receive connected-account status, transaction details, payment identifiers, and other information needed to operate payment features. Payment providers separately process information under their own policies. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Service providers and disclosures

We may share information with providers involved in hosting, payment processing, email, text messaging, fraud prevention, security, and technical operations as needed to deliver the service. We may disclose information when required by law, to address security or fraud, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer.

Messages and communications

Contractors may use email or SMS features to send service-related communications. Message delivery depends on third-party providers. Marketing messages require applicable permissions and opt-out handling; service-related communications should not be used as a substitute for marketing consent.

Cookies and technical data

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for login sessions, preferences, security, performance, and measurement. Third-party services integrated into the platform may use their own technologies. Additional notices or choices may be needed depending on the tracking actually enabled.

Retention and security

We retain information as needed for service delivery, business records, dispute handling, payment reconciliation, security, and legal obligations. We apply reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

Account holders may update certain information in their accounts and may request assistance with access, correction, or deletion. Some records must be retained for accounting, contractual, security, or legal reasons. Contact ContractorSpecialties through the support or contact channel provided by the website or your account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as services and practices change. Material changes may require additional notice or consent where applicable.
